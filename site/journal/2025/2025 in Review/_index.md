Well, here we are at the end of another year! It has been such a strangely varied one that it hardly seems a single year. I think about this often, these days: For all that years do genuinely pass more and more quickly, they nonetheless remain a very substantial block of time. A great many things happen in a year! A rather banal observation that may be, but I *feel* it.

As usual, I am breaking this down into a bunch of different parts focused on different parts of life—both so that you can read only the bits that are interesting to you, and so that I can actually manage to finish the various posts! This series will fill out over the next few days, with relatively brief posts (I think… probably… and emphasis on *relatively*, if past years are anything to go by).

The biggest challenges of the year all came down to the recurring consequences of a nasty injury I sustained late last year: a herniated disc that caused terrible back pain and sciatica off and on over the course of the year. You’ll see that appear as a recurring theme throughout, and I’ll cover it in detail in the post about my health and fitness!
