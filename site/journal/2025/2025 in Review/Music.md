This year I:

- I composed and published [<cite>Holy Saturday</cite>](https://music.chriskrycho.com/works/holy-saturday-2025/), a short chamber work, with versions [for piano](https://chriskrycho.bandcamp.com/track/holy-saturday-version-for-piano); [for violin, viola, cello, and contrabass](https://chriskrycho.bandcamp.com/track/holy-saturday-version-for-violin-viola-cello-and-contrabass); and [for chamber orchestra](https://chriskrycho.bandcamp.com/track/holy-saturday-version-for-chamber-orchestra).
- I finished a first draft of the third movement of the symphony I am writing.
- I wrote [a tiny bit of music](https://soundcloud.com/chriskrycho/on-the-craft) for [my music-focused YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@ChrisKrycho-Music).

See [the corresponding issue](https://newsletter.music.chriskrycho.com/archive/december-2025-how-the-year-went/) of [my music newsletter](https://newsletter.music.chriskrycho.com) for more details and reflection on all of this!
