I just used [Dorico][dorico]’s handy [Exporting Graphic Slices][slices] feature for, I think, the first time, and: what a thoughtful inclusion this is. For one issue of my music newsletter this year (comment on going quarterly some other time—likely the upcoming January–March issue!), I wanted to illustrate, like so:

[dorico]: https://www.steinberg.net/dorico/
[slices]: https://www.steinberg.help/r/dorico-pro/6.1/en/dorico/topics/engrave_mode/engrave_mode_graphic_slices_exporting_t.html

![a very high ledger line](https://cdn.chriskrycho.com/images/very-high-ledger-line.png)

In the past, I might have tried to snag that by using a screenshot utility. Today, I remembered that Dorico has this functionality built in, so I pulled [the aforelinked help page][slices] and followed the instructions there to snag just this view and export it as a high-quality <abbr title="Portable Network Graphics (image format)">PNG</abbr>—with the ability to be much more precise with what I captured and with a good deal more versatility about what to export and how.

Handy!
