But: why? Video takes a ton of time, even when you have [really excellent help][j], and I [noted][n] at the end of last year that it hadn’t done a lot for me in terms of building my music’s audience.

But:

1. I also noticed that people *do* seem to find and watch the Dorico tips I have published, and I really like helping people. If the things I learn while working on this can help others with their own composition, orchestration, etc., *great*.

2. Dorico tips and similar videos *are* a small discovery channel for my music. If someone watches a helpful video about Dorico, they may also look at the other things on my channel. That includes the music I have published.

The first of those is, and must be, the most important. It’s not close. The second is nice if it happens. But I’ll take both if I happen to get them!
