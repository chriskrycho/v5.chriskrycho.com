I read [the Jacobs volume](https://bookshop.org/a/21126/9780199747498) on my Kobo via Libby in July and liked it so much that I had to order a physical copy. (You can look forward to a review soon!)

Meanwhile I’ve had [the Webster volume](https://bookshop.org/a/21126/9780567014252) on my “back catalog” for ages and, inspired by Jacobs’ strong encouragement to read by Whim, went ahead and snagged it. Now I have to resist just throwing aside work and reading Webster all afternoon.
